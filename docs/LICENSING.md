The controlling terms for ZTPU are the exact PolyForm Noncommercial License
1.0.0 text in the repository root `LICENSE` file and the Required Notice in
`NOTICE`. This page is an informal summary and does not replace or expand those
terms.

## Permitted Uses

Subject to the license itself, permitted non-commercial uses include personal
experimentation, learning ZLang HDL, education, academic or non-commercial
research, hobby FPGA projects, architecture study, and modifications made and
distributed for permitted non-commercial purposes.

The license text controls whether a particular organization or use qualifies.

## Commercial Licensing

Commercial use not permitted by the public license requires a separate
commercial license. Depending on the facts and subject to legal review, this
may include incorporation into a commercial FPGA product or ASIC, commercial
deployment, integration into a sold product or service, paid customer work, or
commercial derivative implementations.

For commercial licensing inquiries, open an issue at
<https://github.com/postoroniy/ztpu/issues> without including confidential
information. Commercial terms are provided separately.
